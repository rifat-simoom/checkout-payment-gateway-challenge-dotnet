# Principles

## Aspire

- Customer First: Obsessed with delivering the best client outcomes.
- Run Lean: Putting ROI at the heart of every decision.
- Better Every Day: Learning continuously and avoiding repeated mistakes.
- Never Give Up: Maintaining a resilient, all-in attitude.
- Athlete Mindset: Planning with rigor and setting ambitious goals.

## Excel

- Be The Owner: Taking initiative and accountability.
- No Room For Approximation: Being detail-oriented and data-driven.
- Pace Matters: Acting with urgency and execution speed.
- Talk Straight: Communicating authentically, respectfully, and openly.

## Unite

- One Team: Prioritizing collective success over individual achievements.
- Fundamentally Positive: Fostering trust and compounding positivity.
- Being Together: Thriving through in-person collaboration and celebration.
